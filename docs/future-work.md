## THB2 is untested on hardware

The app speaks THB2 since its port of #4 (`atc/Firmware.kt`), with the Rust tests
ported, but no THB2 device has been connected from a phone yet. Check one with a
read-only action first (Read settings, or Read firmware and calibration, in its menu).
